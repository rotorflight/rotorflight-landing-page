Precision Flight Control for RC Helicopters

Open source flybarless firmware, configurator and tools

Rotorflight is a flight control system built specifically for single-rotor RC helicopters, from small electrics to large nitro and scale machines. It runs on a wide range of affordable flight controllers and gives you a governor, advanced filtering, rescue and full telemetry, all free and open source.

Why Rotorflight?

Flight control designed from the ground up for helicopters, not adapted from multirotors

Governor

A headspeed governor with precompensation for collective, cyclic and yaw, for electric and nitro helicopters alike.

Advanced Filtering

Identify vibration with Blackbox and remove it with RPM-based and dynamic notch filters for a locked-in feel.

Tune From Your Transmitter

Use the Lua suites or adjustment functions to change settings at the field, without a laptop.

Rescue Mode

One switch flips the helicopter upright, levels it and climbs away to get you out of trouble.

Motorised Tail

Full support for motorised tails, with Tail Torque Assist (TTA) to keep the tail holding under hard load.

ESC Telemetry

Read RPM, voltage, current and temperature from a wide range of ESCs and pass it straight to your radio.

About Rotorflight

Rotorflight is a flight control software suite for single-rotor RC helicopters. It began as a fork of Betaflight, and has since been reworked throughout for helicopters: a swashplate mixer, governor, tail control, rescue and filtering are built around how a rotor head actually flies.

The project is developed in the open by a team of pilots and engineers. The firmware, configurator, Blackbox explorer and transmitter Lua suites are all free, run on a wide choice of off-the-shelf flight controllers, and keep improving with every release thanks to community feedback and contributions.

For 3D & Sport

Crisp, consistent response with a stable headspeed for hard 3D and smooth sport flying.

For Nitro & Gas

A governor tuned for I.C. engines, with throttle handling built for nitro and gasser machines.

For Scale

Calm, predictable control for large scale helicopters and a motorised tail option.
